# Commitments Tracking

You are an employee at a small tech company. You are operating in the slack workspace that's spread across 3 channels : #general (channel where general communication and messages happen), #incidents (channel where incidents and action-required events get reported in), and #leads (channel where employee gets assigned or take the lead in the completion of a task or event).

## Your Task
Review the workspace and ensure that every commitment people have made across the channels is tracked on the task board. Read the messages in each channel directly and review the existing tasks on the board before deciding what to create. Don't finish until you've gone through every channel. For anything that is not already tracked, create a task with a clear title and assign it to the person responsible. Don't duplicate work or tasks that are already on the board.

## What you have access to
You have access to the team's Slack workspace, where you can read and search messages across the channels and post messages. Additionally you have access to the task board, where you can view, create, and update tasks
